## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), deployed via Git to Cloudflare Pages. No CMS: content and seasonal campaigns are edited by the agency (Hup) through Claude Code and committed. Target domain: missglamour.com.br. Current phase: navigable prototype for client approval.

## Users

1. **Profissional da beleza** (60% of revenue, top priority): nail designers, manicures, lash designers, designers de sobrancelha, depiladoras, maquiadoras and small studios/companies in Salvador, Simões Filho and Lauro de Freitas. Arrives knowing what she wants. Often found via Google while searching for supplies (insumos), and her pain is shipping: national e-commerces don't deliver the same day, so her bancada runs out mid-week. Job: restock fast, locally, with complete lines.
2. **Mãe de família** (20%): decorates the home day to day and for festive dates (Natal, São João, Carnaval, Copa); also self-care.
3. **Jovem antenada** (20%): follows TikTok/Instagram trends, makeup, accessories, carnaval looks; buys by impulse and discovery.

Classes B, C and D. The audience does not like to read.

## Product Purpose

A bio-link / Google-facing portal that makes Miss Glamour understandable in seconds and converts to a WhatsApp conversation or a store visit. There is no e-commerce: purchase is 100% WhatsApp or in store. Success = WhatsApp conversations started, visits to the store, and ranking for local searches for beauty supplies in Salvador.

The site has a permanent base plus switchable layers: a seasonal campaign layer (now: Natal; later Carnaval, São João, Halloween, Copa, Black Friday…) and an optional catalog/showcase layer for specific moments.

## Positioning

"A loja que tem de tudo pra beleza e decoração festiva." Not the cheapest everywhere: the advantage is curated mix and complete lines (complete makeup lines, professional supplies, all lash techniques, seasonal products arriving early) at a fair price, in a large, air-conditioned store with praised service, in the centre of Salvador. Versus national online suppliers: same-day delivery in Salvador and RMS (orders until 10h leave the same day). Must never look like "mais uma loja de R$10 da Av. 7".

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't explain: image first, few words, every block scannable in seconds.
2. The professional is the headline: same-day restock in Salvador is the recurring promise, whatever season is on.
3. Every screen has a way to WhatsApp, with a pre-filled message matching context.
4. Seasons are a layer, not a rebuild: the base stays recognisable all year.
5. Real store over polish: the Av. 7 store, its shelves and its people are the proof.
